Oldest kanchi record is late 1200s.  
Oldest sringeri maTha record is 1300s. (with some seals slightly earlier?) Given that kanchi was seen as chikka udaiyar while sringeri as pedda udaiyar, sringeri must be as old as kanchi most probably.  
Old place of sringeri was kudali only. And it predates Vidya sankara. Maybe at the current pitha Vidya sankara established. 

Puri sankara matha had no continuous existence. Never in a position to actually retain old texts either.
