Most of the recent "traditionalist" responses to my TA versus NA essay clearly confirm again that there is no recognition of the one fundamental difference between these two perspectives, and this renders any debate futile.

The relentless need to set right against wrong very successfully demonstrates the incomprehension of a message that points to that which is beyond both.

_Tony Parsons, August 2008_
