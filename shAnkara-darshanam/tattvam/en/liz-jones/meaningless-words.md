# Meaningless Words Liz Jones

Source: [TW](https://www.advaita.org.uk/discourses/trad_neo/meaningless_jones.htm)

Author: Liz Jones

The following is a poem from the book 'Simply This' by Liz Jones. It may be purchased directly from her [website](http://www.simplythis.net/), by post (address at website) or from Watkins Bookshop in London.

See the [recommendation](https://www.advaita.org.uk/reading/read_neo.htm#jones) for this book.

Meaningless Words

Everything is dual.  
Nothing is non-dual.  
But nothing appears as everything,  
So what is dual is really non-dual,  
Which means what is dual can't be dual...

Everything is non-dual.  
Nothing is dual.  
But everything appears to be nothing,  
So what's non-dual is really dual,  
Which means that what is non-dual can't be non-dual...
