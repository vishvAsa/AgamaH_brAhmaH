Having bowed to the Lotus Feet of Lakshmi and Hayagriva, we begin 

Vedanta Sangraha for the benefit of the beginners. 

There are two substances: The Seeing (Drik) and the Seen (Drisya); these 

are otherwise known as Intelligence (Chit) and non-intelligence (Achif) and also as 

knowledge and inert matter. The characteristic feature of the substance known as 

the seeing is that it illuminates other substances besides being self-huruinous. 

Knowability is the characteristic feature of the substance"Seen". 

The substance known as the "Sesing" has a four-fold division \-- Brahman 

(Universal Soul), the Lord (Irwara), the Un\-changing (Kutastha) and the Individual 

Soul (Jiva). This division is based, not on any real difference existing between 

them, but on their differing limiting adjuncts (Upadhi). The "Seeing" when coupled 

with Illusion (Maya) it is Brahman, when covered by illusion it is the Invara the 

Lord; when coupled with Ignorance it is the "un\-changing" (Kutaatha), when 

covered with Ignorance it is the "Individual soul (Jiva)". Illusion (Avidya \-Non- 

leaming) is otherwise called the Internal organ (Antah\-Karana). It is also called 

Intellect (Buddhi). Brahman is one and so is the Lord; because their limiting 

adjunct, Viz., Illusion (known also as Nature (Prakriti), Ignorance etc.,) is one. On 

the other hand, the individual soul and the "Unchanging \ (Kutastha) are several, 

for their limiting adjuncts are endless. Some people say that the "Unchanging" (Kutastha) is also one for the reason that its limiting adjunct is of no consequence 

(because it cannot bring about a change in the "unchanging"). 

Brahman is the existing (zar), the Intelligence, and bliss and it is the basis 

for the entire world. The Lord (Tavara) is all-knowing all-powerful all- 

controlling, ever free and is the cause of the creation, sustenance and dissolution 

of the entire world. The "Unchanging " (Kutastha) is the existing, the Intelligence and bliss, and it is sentienos (Chaitanya) having different bodies as its abode. Little (२)

Next, the seen has a two-fold division: effect (Karya) and cause (Karana). 

The osuse called Illusion is of one sort only and as it is this which creates the Lord

foamless is the incorporeal. The incorporeal has four divisions: the senses 

(Indriya); Internal Organ (Antah Karana); elements (Bhuta) and objects (Vishaya).

There are some persons like Vamadeva who had an awakening without
