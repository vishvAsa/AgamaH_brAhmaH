There are two substances: The Seeing (Drik) and the Seen (Drisya); these

Soul (Jiva). This division is based, not on any real difference existing between

them, but on their differing limiting adjuncts (Upadhi). The “Seeing” when coupled

with Illusion (Maya) it is Brahman, when covered by illusion it is the Invara the

Lord; when coupled with Ignorance it is the “un-changing” (Kutaatha), when

covered with Ignorance it is the “Individual soul (Jiva)”. Illusion (Avidya -Non-
